This is a Social Scuba Diving app meant for all Scuba Divers of any level, and also for Professional Dive Centers. The goal is to have an interactive way to log and share your dives with your friends, keep track of the marine species you have found, future dives or dive trips and your diving gear.
You can also interact with your friends via a social feed similar to other social platforms.
As a dive center, you can publish public dive rosters that people can request to join, handle your inventory and warehouse, and handle your planned dives with your employees.
